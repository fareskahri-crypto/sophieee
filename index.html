GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Can I send my own reference?

Absolutely. Use the custom form to preview your image, then attach the same reference when WhatsApp opens.

Are the pieces actually handmade?

Yes. Each piece is shaped, detailed and finished by hand, so tiny variations are part of the charm.

Can I request colors or small changes?

Yes. Describe the colors, expressions, symbols, text or other details you want in the request.

How do I know the price?

Custom requests are quoted after we see the idea, size, detail level and quantity.

Do you make gifts?

Yes. Mention that it is a gift in your request and include any useful occasion or personalization details.
